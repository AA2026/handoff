How it works

Three times by hand. Then it asks.

Nothing to record, describe, or configure. You just start, the way you always do.

  1. You work.

    Copy, switch windows, paste, press return, move down a row. The way you already do it, in whatever apps you already use.

  2. It notices.

    After the second or third pass, the same rhythm shows up in your hands. A small popover appears next to what you're doing.

  3. You say yes.

    It finishes the remaining rows the same way you did the first three. Ignore the popover and it goes away.

The rhythm

It doesn't understand your work. It understands your hands.

It watches four things. That is enough to see a loop, without reading what's on your screen.

  • KeystrokesWhich keys, in what order, how fast.
  • ClipboardWhat kind of thing you copied. A name, an ID, an address.
  • WindowsWhich one is in front, and when you switch.
  • ClicksWhere you click, and whether it's the same spot again.

The work nobody automates, because it's only twenty minutes.

What it isn't

Nothing to set up. Nothing to clean up.

  • Not a macro you record.

    There is no record button. You never start a recording or stop one.

  • Not an agent you prompt.

    You don't describe the task. You've already shown it, by doing it.

  • Not a workflow you build.

    Nothing to configure in advance, and nothing left behind when the batch is done.

It only acts on what you're already doing, right now, in a burst.

Privacy and control

Yours, on your machine.

Everything it sees stays on the computer. Everything it does waits for you.

  • Local only.Keystrokes, clipboard contents, and window titles never leave the machine. There is no account and no server.
  • Waits for you.Every run starts with a popover and ends with your click on yes. It never acts on its own.
  • One key to stop.Press Escape mid-run. It stops at the current row and leaves the finished rows in place.
  • Watchable.It works in the same windows you do, at a pace you can follow. You see every step as it happens.

Details

The questions people ask first.

Which apps does it work with?
Any window on your Mac. It watches input at the system level, so it doesn't need a plugin or an integration for each app.
How many times do I have to do something before it offers?
Two or three. It needs to see the same sequence repeat, with the same shape of data moving through it, before it will ask.
What if it's wrong about the pattern?
Click no, or keep working. It only ever asks. If it misreads a step mid-run, press Escape and fix the row by hand.
Does it run in the background all day?
It watches for rhythm while it's on, but it only speaks up during a burst. If you're not repeating yourself, you won't hear from it.
